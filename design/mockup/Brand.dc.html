The story

The Sebeto is the ancient river of Naples. The city was built over it, yet it still flows underground, unseen, keeping Naples alive. Parthenope founded the city; the Sebeto keeps it alive.

Sebeto does the same for your work: you speak in plain words, and a team of AI agents keeps it flowing beneath the surface. It comes up only when it needs you.

Voice

We talk like a helpful colleague from Naples: warm, direct, never technical.
Say
“The Comms writer needs your OK before emailing staff.”
“Done. 84 runbooks sorted by system.”
“Speak, and your team gets to work.”
Don’t say
“Tool call requires elevated permission.”
“Process exited with code 0.”
“Orchestrate multi-agent pipelines.”
Characters (proposed): Munaciello, the Chief of Staff who takes your requests · Presepi, ready-made agent team templates.
