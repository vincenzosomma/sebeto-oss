# Code of conduct

Sebeto follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, give and take feedback kindly, and focus on what is best for the people who
use Sebeto. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or
anywhere else the project is represented.

## Reporting
If someone's behaviour worries you, contact the maintainers privately through GitHub (for example, on the
maintainer's profile), not in a public issue. Every report is read, handled in confidence, and answered.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban people who break it
repeatedly, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
